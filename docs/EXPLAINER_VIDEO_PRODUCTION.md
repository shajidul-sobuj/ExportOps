## 2. Style Guide & Visual Rules

- **Character (Rafi):** Muscular stickman with round head, simple bold outline body, defined biceps/triceps, pectorals, and broad shoulders. Wears a yellow factory hard hat and a high-vis safety vest with deep teal (`#0d9488`) accent and silver reflective stripes.
- **Canvas Aesthetic:** Ultra-clean light gray / white background (`#f8fafc`), thick dark vector contours (`#0f172a`), flat modern colors.
- **Color System:**
  - **Brand Accent:** Deep Teal (`#0d9488` / `#0f766e`)
  - **Blocked / Critical:** Crimson Red (`#ef4444` / `#b91c1c`)
  - **At Risk / Warning:** Amber Gold (`#f59e0b` / `#d97706`)
  - **Customs-Ready / Success:** Emerald Green (`#10b981` / `#059669`)
  - **Neutral High-Contrast:** Slate Ink (`#0f172a`) on White (`#ffffff`)
- **Animation Motion:** Simple, snappy, punchy moves: slide-in, pop-up, screen-shake on stamp, stamp slam. No cluttered 3D transitions.
- **Audio Mix:**
  - **Bed:** Light, modern upbeat lo-fi tech groove that dips low/tense at 0:12, and lifts triumphantly at 0:42 (ExportOps reveal).
  - **SFX:** Phone vibration buzz (`0:06`), rubber stamp thud (`0:08`), document pop pings (`0:14–0:28`), paper rustle, soft mouse clicks, success chime (`1:22`, `2:45`).
- **Voiceover Delivery:** Calm, authoritative, crisp documentary tone; zero hyperbole or frantic sales pitch.

## 3. Scene-by-Scene Production Script

### Scene 1 · 0:00 to 0:12 · The Stuck Shipment (12 seconds)
- **Visual:** Rafi stands proudly beside a tall stack of labeled garment export cartons at a Dhaka factory loading dock. A heavy 40ft container is loaded onto a trailer in the background. Suddenly, his phone buzzes with a notification. A giant, red rubber stamp slams diagonally across the container with screen-shake: **`SHIPMENT HELD`**. Rafi’s proud expression drops into instant shock.
- **On-Screen Text (Overlay):** `12,000 garments. Ready on time. Stuck.`
- **Voiceover (28 words · 10.5s speech + 1.5s pause):**
  > *"Meet Rafi. His factory finished twelve thousand garments right on time. But at the port, his shipment is stuck. Not because of the product. Because of paperwork."*
- **Sound Design:** Warm upbeat background guitar/synth starts. Phone vibration buzz at `0:06`. Heavy rubber stamp thud + bass drop at `0:08`. Music drops to a low, tense hum.

---

### Scene 2 · 0:12 to 0:30 · Everything That Goes Wrong (18 seconds)
- **Visual:** Chaotic export papers swirl around a sweating Rafi. 5 distinct failure points pop up sequentially with flashing red warning rings:
  1. `0:12 – 0:17`: Commercial Invoice (`12,000 pcs`) side-by-side with Packing List (`11,400 pcs`) with a `-600 pcs` red diff badge.
  2. `0:17 – 0:21`: LC calendar icon with the latest shipment cutoff crossed out, actual vessel date sailing 2 days late.
  3. `0:21 – 0:24`: Document folder with Certificate of Origin (COO) shown as an empty glowing slot with a red `MISSING` tag.
  4. `0:24 – 0:27`: Fabric roll stack with Utilization Declaration (UD) quota meter glowing red: `UD NOT MATCHED (+350 kg excess)`.
  5. `0:27 – 0:30`: Three heavy stone cost tags drop onto Rafi's shoulders: `DEMURRAGE DELAY`, `BANK QUERY`, `LATE PAYMENT`.
- **On-Screen Text:** `Quantity Mismatch` • `LC Expired` • `Missing COO` • `UD Quota Breach` • `$8,500 Demurrage`
- **Voiceover (38 words · 16s speech + 2s pause):**
  > *"The invoice says twelve thousand pieces. The packing list says eleven thousand four hundred. The LC date has passed. A certificate is missing. And the bonded fabric doesn't match the UD. Each mismatch means delay, bank queries, and late payment."*
- **Sound Design:** 5 crisp, rhythmic warning pings (`0:13`, `0:18`, `0:22`, `0:25`, `0:28`). Music stays low, tense, and ticking.

- **Voiceover (27 words · 10.5s speech + 1.5s pause):**

- **Sound Design:** Paper rustle sounds, keyboard typing clicks, muted thud as the post-filing query rejects the shipment.

### Scene 5 · 0:50 to 1:25 · Rafi's Problem, Solved (35 seconds — Core Product Demo)
- **Visual Sequence (Synchronized with real prototype screens):**
  - `0:50 – 0:55`: Prototype screen showing **Shipments List (`/app/shipments`)**. Rafi drags 5 PDF documents into the drop-zone for `EXP-001 (Blocked)`.
  - `0:55 – 1:02`: Dual-window **Inspection Studio (`/app/shipments/EXP-001`)** extracts key fields with 99% confidence badges and clickable green source citations. Rafi watches, visibly impressed.
  - `1:02 – 1:10`: The **8-Point Deterministic Rules Engine** evaluates live. Green checkmarks light up for R2, R3, R4, R6, R7 while R1 (Qty) and R8 (Bond) show yellow/red indicators. Rafi nods, arms crossed.
  - `1:10 – 1:17`: The **Exceptions Panel** explains the 3 root causes in plain English: *"Packing list carton #42 missing 600 pcs; Fabric consumption exceeds UD #4410 by 200 kg"*. Rafi points at each explanation.
  - `1:17 – 1:22`: Rafi types on his laptop and clicks **`Simulate 1-Click Reconcile (+600 PCS)`**. The numbers instantly match: `12,000 / 12,000 PCS (100%)`.
  - `1:22 – 1:25`: Status badge dramatically flips from red **`BLOCKED`** to glowing emerald **`CUSTOMS-READY`** with readiness score leaping to **`100/100`**. A cryptographic QR Gate Pass is generated. Rafi pumps his fist; in the background, the container ship blows its horn and departs on schedule.
- **On-Screen Text:** `Read. Check. Explain. Fix.` ➔ `Blocked to Customs-Ready.`
- **Voiceover (62 words · 29s speech + 6s pause):**
  > *"Rafi uploads his documents. ExportOps reads them in seconds, and shows where each value came from. Eight rules check every document against the others, and against the bond ledger. Three problems appear, each explained in plain language. Rafi fixes them and re-runs. Blocked turns to customs-ready, and his container sails."*
- **Sound Design:** Clean UI clicks, scanning hum, paper drop sound, and a resonant crystal-clear success chime as the status flips green at `1:22`.
